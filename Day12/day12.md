# Day 12 – Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit

## 📌 Overview

As part of the **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**, Day 12 focused on using Claude AI to build a personalized **Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit** based on my resume, target role, skills, projects, and professional background.

My target role for this task is **Data Analytics / Entry-Level Data Analyst**.

The objective was to transform my existing profile into recruiter-focused, ATS-friendly, and ready-to-use job search materials.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this task were to:

* Analyze my resume and professional profile.
* Identify my strongest technical and professional skills.
* Highlight relevant projects, certifications, and experience.
* Identify skill gaps for a Data Analytics career.
* Create personalized recruiter and hiring-manager communication.
* Build a professional personal brand.
* Prepare interview talking points.
* Improve my job-search strategy using AI.

---

## 🛠️ Process Followed

1. Uploaded my latest resume to Claude.
2. Selected **Data Analytics / Data Analyst** as the target career direction.
3. Provided a relevant Job Description.
4. Used the **Job Search & Personal Branding Prompt**.
5. Generated a complete personalized job-search toolkit.
6. Reviewed the ATS recommendations and skill-gap analysis.
7. Ran the second improvement prompt.
8. Saved the generated outputs.
9. Captured screenshots of important sections.

## 🧠 Key Learnings

### 1. Resume customization matters

A single generic resume may not be equally effective for every job. The resume and communication should be aligned with the requirements of the specific role.

### 2. Skills should be connected to outcomes

Simply listing Python, SQL, Power BI, or Excel is less effective than demonstrating how these skills were applied in projects to solve problems or generate useful insights.

### 3. ATS optimization is important

Using relevant job-specific keywords and clearly presenting technical skills can improve the discoverability of a resume during the initial screening process.

### 4. Personal branding supports job search

A consistent professional introduction, LinkedIn presence, recruiter messaging, and positioning statement can make communication more focused and professional.

### 5. Skill-gap analysis provides direction

Identifying missing or developing skills makes it easier to create a focused learning roadmap instead of learning technologies randomly.

### 6. Projects are important for an entry-level profile

For an early-career Data Analytics profile, practical projects can demonstrate the ability to apply technical knowledge beyond coursework.

### 7. AI can support career preparation

Claude can be used not only for resume writing but also for analyzing job requirements, identifying gaps, improving professional communication, and preparing interview talking points.
