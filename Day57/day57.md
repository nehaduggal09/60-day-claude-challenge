# 💡 Problem Statement

When people experience symptoms, they may not know:

- Which medical specialist they should consult
- Whether their symptoms may require urgent attention
- Where nearby healthcare facilities are located
- What basic precautions they can take while seeking professional care

SymptomCare aims to provide a simple starting point for navigating these questions.

> **Important:** SymptomCare is an informational assistant and does not replace professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

---

# 🌟 Core Features

## 🩺 Symptom-Based Assistance

Users can enter their symptoms and receive AI-generated healthcare guidance.

The application focuses on:

- Possible health conditions
- Recommended medical specialist
- General precautions
- Emergency warning information

## 👨‍⚕️ Specialist Recommendation

Based on the user's symptoms, SymptomCare can suggest a relevant specialist category.

Examples:

- Cardiologist
- Dermatologist
- Neurologist
- Orthopedic Specialist
- General Physician
- ENT Specialist
- Gastroenterologist

## 🚨 Emergency Awareness

The application highlights situations where symptoms may require urgent medical attention.

The system encourages users to seek professional help instead of relying solely on AI-generated information.

## 🌍 Language Support

SymptomCare is designed to make healthcare assistance easier to understand for a wider range of users.

Current language support:

- English
- Hindi

The architecture can be extended to additional languages in future versions.

## 📍 Nearby Healthcare Facilities

Users can discover nearby:

- Clinics
- Hospitals
- Healthcare facilities

The location-based experience helps users move from symptom understanding toward real-world healthcare access.
