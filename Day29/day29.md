# ⚡ Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

> An interactive, gamified supply chain crisis simulator built with React, designed to help beginners understand real-world supply chain decision-making through hands-on scenarios.

## 🚨 Overview

**Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab** puts you in the role of a supply chain executive managing a fictional company's crisis.

Every simulation generates a different:

- 🏢 Company
- 🌍 Global footprint
- 🚨 Supply chain crisis
- 📊 Business conditions
- 💰 Financial trade-offs
- 🤝 Supplier negotiation
- 🤖 AI investment strategy
- 📈 Final performance score

The goal is not simply to choose the cheapest option. You must balance **cost, inventory, delivery speed, profitability, risk, supplier relationships, and customer satisfaction**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Your mission is to successfully navigate a supply chain disruption while protecting the business.

You will:

1. Understand the company's supply chain
2. Analyze the crisis
3. Choose three emergency response actions
4. Negotiate with a strategic supplier
5. Make CEO-level leadership decisions
6. Select two AI investments
7. Review your final crisis performance

---

## 🧩 Simulation Flow

### 1. 🏁 Welcome Screen

Start the simulation from a clean task-focused interface.

The simulation explains that you are responsible for protecting the company's operations during a major disruption.

---

### 2. 🏢 Company & Crisis Generation

A fictional company is randomly generated with information such as:

- Company name
- Industry
- Annual revenue
- Number of factories
- Number of warehouses
- Number of suppliers
- Inventory coverage
- Supplier lead time
- Countries served

A random crisis is then introduced.

### Possible Crises

- 🔥 Factory Fire
- 🏦 Supplier Bankruptcy
- ⚓ Port Strike
- 🛡️ Cyberattack
- 🌊 Regional Flood
- ⛏️ Raw Material Shortage
- 🌐 Political Conflict
- 🚢 Shipping Delay

Each crisis has its own:

- Urgency
- Business impact
- Primary risk
- Operational pressure

---

## 3. ⚔️ War Room

You receive six possible emergency actions but can select only **three**.

### Available Actions

- ✈️ Expedite Critical Shipments
- 🔄 Activate Backup Suppliers
- 📦 Release Safety Stock
- 🛣️ Reroute Distribution
- 🎯 Prioritize Key Customers
- ⏸️ Pause Low-Priority Orders

Every action has trade-offs.

The simulator updates:

- 💰 Cost Pressure
- 📦 Inventory Protection
- 📈 Profit Protection
- 🚚 Delivery Speed
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction

### Why This Matters

Supply chain decisions rarely have a perfect answer.

Reducing delivery time may increase transportation cost.

Using safety stock may protect customers today but reduce your future buffer.

The simulator teaches you to think in terms of **trade-offs instead of isolated decisions**.

---

## 4. 🤝 Supplier Negotiation

The negotiation stage contains **four rounds**.

You must respond to changing supplier situations involving:

- Delivery capacity
- Pricing
- Long-term commitments
- Performance expectations
- Crisis collaboration

Your decisions influence:

- 🤝 Supplier Trust
- 💵 Price Pressure
- ⏱️ Lead-Time Pressure
- 🏆 Negotiation Score

Each decision provides immediate educational feedback explaining why the choice was strong or weak.

### Key Learning

A supplier relationship is more than a purchasing contract.

During a crisis, trust can become an operational advantage.

---

## 5. 🧠 CEO Boardroom

You then face five leadership questions.

The questions test your ability to:

- Prioritize critical customers
- Make decisions with incomplete information
- Evaluate emergency spending
- Handle supplier accountability
- Learn from disruptions

Your performance generates a **Leadership Score**.

The goal is to demonstrate executive thinking rather than simply choosing the most obvious answer.

---

## 6. 🤖 AI Strategy

You can select **two AI investments** from five options.

### AI Investment Options

| Investment | Primary Benefit |
|---|---|
| 📈 Demand Forecasting | Better demand prediction |
| 📦 Inventory Optimization | Better inventory allocation |
| 🛰️ Supplier Risk Monitoring | Earlier disruption warnings |
| 👁️ Warehouse Vision | Better warehouse visibility |
| 🤖 Procurement Copilot | Faster procurement decisions |

Your selections influence:

- Resilience
- Cost Control
- Risk Management
- Customer Satisfaction

The simulator encourages users to connect AI investments to actual business outcomes instead of adopting AI simply because it is fashionable.

---

# 📊 Final Dashboard

At the end of the simulation, you receive an **Overall Crisis Score from 0–100**.

### Performance Metrics

- 🧠 Leadership
- 🤝 Negotiation
- 🛡️ Resilience
- 💰 Cost Control
- ⚠️ Risk Management
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction

The final report also provides personalized feedback:

### ⚠️ Biggest Mistake

Identifies a major weakness in your decision-making.

### 🏆 Best Decision

Highlights one of your strongest strategic choices.

### 🧠 Expert Recommendation

Provides a supply-chain-focused recommendation for improving future resilience.

### 📚 Lessons Learned

Summarizes practical lessons from the simulation.
