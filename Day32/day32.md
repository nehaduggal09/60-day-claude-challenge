# 🧠 Think Like a Marketing Strategist: Grow This Brand

An interactive, beginner-friendly marketing strategy simulator built with **React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

The goal is not simply to generate marketing content — it teaches users **how marketers think, analyze a brand, understand an audience, make platform decisions, build content strategy, and respond to unexpected marketing situations.**

---

## 🚀 Overview

**Think Like a Marketing Strategist: Grow This Brand** is a replayable marketing strategy learning experience where users take the role of a marketing strategist.

Instead of immediately creating social media posts, the simulator walks the user through the strategic thinking process:

> **Understand → Analyze → Choose → Plan → Respond → Learn**

Users can practice with:

- Their own business
- Their personal brand
- A randomly generated client/business

The simulator provides explanations throughout the experience so beginners understand **what they are doing and why the decision matters.**

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing the simulation, users should understand how to:

- Understand a business before marketing it
- Define and analyze a target audience
- Identify audience needs and pain points
- Select appropriate social media platforms
- Build content pillars
- Prioritize marketing goals
- Create a strategic 30-day roadmap
- Respond to unexpected marketing events
- Evaluate marketing decisions
- Identify mistakes and opportunities
- Think strategically instead of simply generating content
- Use Claude effectively through reusable prompts

# 🧱 Content Pillars

The simulator generates multiple possible content pillars.

Users must choose **exactly three**.

Examples:

### Educational Content

Helps the audience learn something useful.

**Goal:** Authority + trust

### Thought Leadership

Shares opinions, frameworks, predictions, and unique perspectives.

**Goal:** Positioning + credibility

### Personal Story

Uses experiences and lessons to create connection.

**Goal:** Authenticity + relationship

### Behind the Scenes

Shows how the work happens.

**Goal:** Transparency + trust

### Product / Service Education

Explains how the offering solves customer problems.

**Goal:** Conversion

### Audience Community

Encourages conversation and participation.

**Goal:** Engagement + loyalty

The simulator explains how each pillar contributes to different marketing goals.

# 🤖 Learn How to Ask Claude

After every major section, the simulator includes a:

## "How to ask Claude" Card

Each card contains a reusable prompt that teaches users how to use Claude for strategic thinking.

The goal is not simply to give users answers.

It teaches them how to **write better prompts.**

Prompts can include:

- Business analysis prompts
- Audience research prompts
- Platform strategy prompts
- Content pillar prompts
- 30-day strategy prompts
- Crisis-response prompts
- Personal branding prompts

---

## 🙋 Personal Brand Prompt Adaptation

When Personal Brand mode is selected, Claude prompts reference:

- The person's name
- Their niche
- Their expertise
- Their audience
- Their personal story

instead of referring to a company.

Example structure:

> "Act as a personal branding strategist. Analyze [NAME]'s expertise in [NICHE] and identify the audience they should focus on, their strongest positioning opportunity, and three content themes that can establish authority while remaining authentic."

This helps users understand how prompts should be adapted to context.
