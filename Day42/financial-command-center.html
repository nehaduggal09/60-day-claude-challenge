Quick money tips for students

Give every rupee a "job" as soon as it arrives — even irregular allowance can go into envelopes the same day you get it.
Track small purchases too. Snacks and impulse buys are usually the biggest silent leak in a student budget.
Aim to move something into savings before you spend on wants — even ₹20 a week builds the habit.
Review your log weekly, not daily — it's less pressure and still keeps you honest.
