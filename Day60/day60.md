# 🩺 SymptomCare

### AI-Powered Healthcare Assistant | 10-Day Capstone

SymptomCare is an AI-powered healthcare assistant designed to help users describe symptoms, understand possible health concerns, identify a relevant medical specialist, receive emergency guidance, and discover nearby clinics or hospitals.

Built as the **10-Day Capstone Sprint** of the **AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge**.
