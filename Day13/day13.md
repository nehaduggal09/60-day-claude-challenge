I am interested in industries where data analysis and technology are actively used for decision-making, reporting, business insights, and process improvement.

## Jobs to Exclude

Exclude opportunities that:

* Require significantly more professional experience than my current level.
* Are unrelated to Data Analytics, Data Science, Business Analytics, or closely related areas.
* Primarily focus on technologies outside my target career direction.
* Require qualifications that I do not currently possess.
* Do not provide meaningful alignment with my career goals.

## Job Posting Recency

Prioritize **recently posted opportunities**, especially jobs posted within the last few days or weeks, so that I can focus on active and relevant openings.

## Additional Preferences

* Prioritize entry-level and internship opportunities suitable for a B.Tech student.
* Prioritize roles where my existing Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and data-analysis skills are relevant.
* Prefer opportunities that provide practical exposure to real-world datasets and business problems.
* Prioritize companies offering learning, mentorship, and career-growth opportunities.
* Give preference to roles where my academic projects, open-source experience, certifications, and technical skills can strengthen my application.
* Prioritize opportunities with clear job responsibilities and transparent requirements.
* Focus on roles that can help me build a long-term career in Data Analytics.

My overall goal is to secure an entry-level **Data Analyst / Data Analytics** opportunity where I can apply my current technical and analytical skills, gain real-world industry experience, strengthen my professional profile, and grow toward advanced Data Analytics and Data Science roles.
